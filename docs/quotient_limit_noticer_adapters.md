# QuotientLimit Noticer Adapters

K9-QL-11 defines a public-only boundary for converting AETS, ATv2 scheduling, APLOT superframes, AEPA leases, Menfugu execution slots, QuotientForge transducers, and accepted QuotientSeal manifests into AQRP candidates.

Every source is bound to nonzero source and policy digests. Public transitions must be canonical and remain within declared state, quotient, public-input, fault-input, and horizon axes. PPG, personal baseline, raw biosignal, and private history fields are rejected before conversion.

The comparison registry fixes M0 through M7. Exact rational reporting returns actual cost, certified lower bound, absolute optimality gap, and relative gap using an explicit positive scale floor.

Adapters preserve public semantics; they do not claim that a marginal or unverified source is privacy-safe, optimal, or equivalent to its originating implementation.
