# Robust Fault and Multi-Service Collusion

K9-QL-08 adds exact candidate evaluation for public fault scenarios and joint service observations.

- Robust utility is required for every scenario explicitly marked recoverable.
- Worst-case cost and expectation under an explicit normalized public prior are separate objectives.
- The frozen gate requires at least four distinct fault families.
- Collusion is measured on the joint service trace, not inferred from marginal privacy.
- Independent, shared, and correlated randomness are explicit mechanism annotations.
- Correlation leakage is the excess of joint total variation over the maximum marginal total variation.

Erasure and FEC lower bounds remain classical coding-theory results. This module only combines public fault recovery, action utility, and privacy evaluation; it does not claim those bounds as new.
