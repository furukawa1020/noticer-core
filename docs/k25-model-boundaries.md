# Comparing the K24 and K25 scopes

K24 quantifies over fixed but initially unrevealed public future tapes. Its
policy sees the public observation prefix and need not meet a trace privacy
constraint. The outcome is deadline success.

K25 fixes exactly two histories and makes their eligible slots known to the
trusted planner. Its observer sees a specified projection of declared logical
receipts. Matching the two unconditional laws under that projection is a
constraint, not a side effect of obtaining the same delivery rate.

Neither model implies the other. In particular, K25 can withhold actions to
match traces, but it does not synthesize one online policy that handles
unknown futures. K24 delivering an action by its deadline does not imply
that its receipt trace is independent of private input. Both evaluate
mathematical fixtures, not any particular live transport.
